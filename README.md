# sales-data-analysis

## Objective
Analyze a retail sales dataset using SQL to identify revenue trends and time-based performance

## Tools
- SQL
- Kaggle dataset

## Dataset
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kyanyoga/sample-sales-data/data
- Contains transactional sales records including:
  - Order date
  - Sales amount
  - Country
  - Product and customer information
 
## Analysis Overview
### 1. Sales by Country and Year
Analyzed total revenue by country across different years to identify geographic performance trends.
Purpose:
- Compare performance across regions
- Identify top-performing countries per year

### 2. Daily Cumulative Sales (Monthly Trend)
Calculated cumulative daily sales within a month to understand revenue progression over time.
Purpose:
- Track how revenue accumulates throughout a month
- Identify patterns such as slow starts or end-of-month spikes

### 3. Top 3 Monthly Sales per Year
Identified the highest-performing months for each year using ranking functions.
Purpose:
- Highlight peak sales periods
- Support seasonal trend analysis
